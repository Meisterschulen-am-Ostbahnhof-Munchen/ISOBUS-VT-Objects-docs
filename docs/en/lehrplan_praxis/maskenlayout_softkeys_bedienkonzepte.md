# 📱 Mask Layout, Softkeys & Ergonomics

> 📌 **Source & Legal Basis:**
> **Source:** *Framework Curriculum for the Preparation for the Master Craftsman Examination in Agricultural and Construction Machinery Mechatronics*
> **Publisher:** German Association of Agricultural and Construction Machinery Mechatronics Technicians (LandBauTechnik-Bundesverband e. V.), Alfredstraße 102, 45131 Essen (as of February 25, 2025)

> **Regulation:** *Master Craftsman Examination Regulation (LandBauMechMstrV)* of September 9, 2024 (Federal Law Gazette 2024 I No. 277, effective August 1, 2025)

**Framework Curriculum Category:** Part I LE 1.4 | Part II LE 1.4 (80 teaching units)

**Relevance:** Ergonomic design of machine displays and intuitive softkey hierarchies.

---

## 1. Data Mask Design

- **Object Placement:** Structuring of output values (numerical values, bar graphs, graphics, icons).

- **Color Tables & Scaling:** Use of standard color codes according to ISO 11783-6 for status indicators (Green = Normal Operation, Yellow = Warning, Red = Error).

- **Dynamic Hide/Show:** Hiding inactive controls to prevent driver distraction.

---

## 2. Softkey Structures

- **Physical vs. Virtual Keys:** Mapping of on-screen buttons to physical buttons on the terminal frame or joystick (AUX-N / AUX-O).

- **Softkey Levels:** Hierarchical navigation between the main menu, settings, and diagnostic screens.
